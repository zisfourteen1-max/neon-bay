Open-world crime game — explore Neon Bay on foot and by car, steal cars, outrun the police and run missions.

You are Kit Marlowe, fresh out of the county lockup with $120 and a debt to pay. Vera Voss runs the bay from a back room above Ray's Garage — do her four jobs, steal what you need, and stay ahead of the Neon Bay police as your wanted level climbs from one star to five. The city is yours to explore: traffic on the avenues, pedestrians on the sidewalks, Palm Park in the middle, a beach along the south shore and docks on the east edge. Progress saves automatically.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser.

Controls: WASD/arrows move · mouse aim · click shoot · E steal / exit car · Space punch / handbrake · R reload · M mute · P pause.
